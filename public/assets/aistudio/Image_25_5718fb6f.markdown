## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-trust, discreet, and empowering male preventive reproductive healthcare experience. It departs from sterile hospital tropes and aggressive hyper-masculine aesthetics, leaning into modern clinical longevity, restorative biology, and quantified vitality (channeling references like Oura, Levels, and modern functional wellness).

The aesthetic blends **Modern Minimalist Healthcare** with **Tactile Biometric Luxury**:
- **Tone:** Methodical, discreet, scientific yet emotionally grounding, and reassuringly sophisticated.
- **Visual Personality:** Airy warm canvas, deep botanical greens representing regenerative vitality, and vivid biomonitor accents that transform private health data into confident, actionable habits.
- **Target Audience:** Men proactively optimizing reproductive health, hormonal balance, lifestyle factors, and long-term vitality.

## Colors
The palette balances grounded clinical authority with biological vitality and calming warm negative space.

- **Primary Anchor (`#123D32` - Deep Forest Green):** The brand’s grounding authority. Used for primary interactive actions, high-emphasis icons, hero headlines, and authoritative status states.
- **Vitality Accent (`#5CBA83` - Fresh Vitality Green):** Used selectively for biometric scoring meters, habit streaks, trend indicators, active toggle thumbs, and progress fills.
- **Highlight Tint (`#EAF6EE` - Soft Mint):** Serves as subtle backgrounds for clinical tags, streak badges, pill chips, and secondary active containers.
- **Page Canvas (`#FAF9F4` - Warm Ivory):** Replaces harsh sterile white on the main background to reduce eye fatigue and deliver an organic, comfortable wellness atmosphere.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Reserved for elevated cards, modal sheets, and interactive input containers to provide crisp physical contrast against the warm ivory background.
- **Typography Charcoal (`#202B29`):** Provides sharp, accessible contrast for body copy and headings without the clinical harshness of pitch black.
- **Structural Stroke (`#E6E4DD` - Warm Sand Border):** Used for non-intrusive container edges, segmented dividers, and discrete card borders.

## Typography
The system balances human warmth with precise laboratory clarity. 

- **Headlines & Scores (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with balanced geometric curves and slightly tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain a contemporary, premium digital clinic presence.
- **Body & Data Readings (Inter):** Highly legible at small scales, delivering fatigue-free reading for daily routine checklists, ingredient breakdowns, biomarker lab interpretations, and personalized clinical guidance.
- **Metrics & Numbers (`metric-display`):** Optimized for single-glance bio-scores (sperm motility index, hormonal baseline trends, supplement consistency scores).
- **Caps & Micro Labels (`label-sm`):** Set with slight positive letter spacing (`0.04em`) to maintain legibility on status badges, certified seals, and streak pills.

## Layout & Spacing
A fluid 4-column layout on mobile scales to 8 columns on tablet and 12 columns on desktop web views, anchored by a strict 4px/8px modular spacing cadence.

- **Mobile Viewports (under 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margins. Ensures touch targets comfortably stay over 48px while maintaining room for breathing whitespace around cards.
- **Tablet / Large Screens (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Content cards form clean 2-up grid modules.
- **Desktop Dashboard (1024px+):** Max-width bounded at 1200px centered on `#FAF9F4`, with 12 columns allowing comprehensive multi-panel wellness tracking (e.g., protocol roadmap beside biometric history).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics clean architectural layers rather than harsh dropped dropshadows. Surfaces build upon each other through light, ambient occlusion, and subtle borderline definitions.

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Warm Ivory (`#FAF9F4`), completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Action Tiles):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, bounded by a hairline stroke (`1px solid #E6E4DD`), cast over by an ultra-soft botanical ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(18, 61, 50, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(32, 43, 41, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Navigation & Modals):** Crisp white surface with increased blur and slight elevation: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(18, 61, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(18, 61, 50, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Alerts & Health Action Toasts):** Pure White with accent border or deep `#123D32` surface with high-contrast vitality text and deeper spread shadow.

## Shapes
The shape language combines welcoming organic geometry with modern product sophistication:
- **Default Structural Containers (`rounded-2xl` / 1rem - 1.25rem):** Used for primary metric panels, lab result modules, and daily protocol cards.
- **High-Impact Hero Cards (`rounded-3xl` / 1.5rem - 1.75rem):** Used for biomarker summary cards, readiness rings, and lifestyle onboarding modals.
- **Action Buttons & Badges (Fully Pill-Rounded / 9999px):** Applied to primary CTAs, habit streak chips, protocol tags, and biometric pill counters for a tactile, friendly aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Pill-shaped, background `#123D32`, text `#FFFFFF`, 48px height on mobile. Subtle active scale effect (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary / Vitality Action:** Pill-shaped, background `#5CBA83`, text `#123D32` (or white when contrast mandates), used for positive daily completion (e.g., "Complete Protocol", "Log Dose").
- **Tertiary / Discrete:** Background `#EAF6EE`, text `#123D32`, border `1px solid transparent`. Hover transitions to border `#5CBA83`.

### Metric Rings & Interactive Gauges
- **Circular Progress Track:** Unfilled ring `#E6E4DD` with stroke width of 8px to 12px.
- **Active Progress Indicator:** Gradient stroke from `#123D32` to `#5CBA83` with rounded caps. Center displays `metric-display` in `#202B29` with a subtle `label-sm` unit descriptor.

### Cards & Wellness Modules
- **Base Surface:** Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded `rounded-2xl`, border `1px solid #E6E4DD`.
- **Padding:** 20px (`1.25rem`) internal padding.
- **Header:** Accompanied by micro-icons enclosed in `#EAF6EE` circular badges with `#123D32` iconography.

### Habit Streak & Medical Safety Badges
- **Streak Pill:** Pill-shaped, background `#EAF6EE`, text `#123D32`, accompanied by a small `#5CBA83` flame or checkmark icon. Font `label-sm`.
- **Clinical / HIPAA Safety Seal:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E6E4DD`, text `#202B29` with an integrated `#123D32` lock or medical cross emblem.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Field:** Height 52px, pure white surface, border `1px solid #E6E4DD`, corner radius 12px. Placeholder text `#8A9490`. On focus: border `1.5px solid #123D32` with an ambient glow of `rgba(92, 186, 131, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Habit Toggles:** 24px rounded-lg squares or pill segmented switches. Unchecked: border `1.5px solid #E6E4DD`, checked: background `#5CBA83`, white check icon.
